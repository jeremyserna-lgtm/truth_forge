# Alta Mile High: Job Interview Briefing for Leasing Agent Role

## Executive Summary

Alta Mile High (AMH) is a newly developed luxury apartment community located at 1450 Morrison Rd in Denver’s emerging Sun Valley neighborhood. Managed by Greystar in partnership with developer Wood Partners, the property distinguishes itself through a unique music and arts-focused brand identity, accessible luxury pricing, and a strategic location near the Light Rail.

The primary objective for a leasing agent at this property is to convert prospects by selling a "growth narrative." Because AMH is located in a transitional neighborhood, the role requires a sophisticated ability to handle objections regarding location and walkability by emphasizing superior value, authentic culture, and future neighborhood appreciation. Success in the interview will depend on demonstrating an understanding of the property's four pillars of differentiation: Cultural Identity, Value Proposition, Transit-Enabled Access, and the Morrison Road Growth Corridor.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------


## I. Property Profile and Strategic Positioning

### Core Property Data

• **Address:** 1450 Morrison Rd, Denver, CO 80204.

• **Units:** Studio, 1BR, 2BR, 3BR.

• **Pricing:** 11% below area average; 1BRs starting in the low $1,300s (net effective).

• **Key Concession:** Up to 10 weeks of free base rent (a primary tool for rapid lease-up).

• **Branding:** Music, arts, and culture focus (e.g., vinyl aesthetics, "Now spinning at AMH").

### The Four Pillars of Differentiation

To effectively lease at AMH, an agent must master these four selling points:

1. **Cultural Identity (Music + Arts):** Unlike generic luxury properties, AMH uses vinyl record aesthetics and artist showcases to create an emotional connection. It is positioned as a "community with a soul."

2. **Value Proposition (Accessible Luxury):** AMH offers the same high-end finishes (quartz countertops, stainless steel, wood-style flooring) as downtown properties but at a significant discount—often 300–500 less per month.

3. **Transit-Enabled Urban Access:** Located a 5-minute walk from the Decatur-Federal Light Rail station (W/D Lines), providing a 15–20 minute commute to Downtown Denver and easy access to the Sports Complex (Empower Field, Ball Arena).

4. **Morrison Road Growth Corridor:** AMH is a first-mover in a redevelopment zone. Prospective residents are invited to "invest" in where the neighborhood is going, rather than paying a premium for where it already is.

## III. Leasing Agent Competencies and Objection Handling

### Core Professional Traits

Based on an analysis of the candidate's personal profile (Haze) and the property's needs, the following traits should be highlighted during the interview:

• **Directness and Curiosity:** The ability to ask prospects direct questions to uncover their true needs.

• **Presence:** Being a "calm" presence who can sit with a prospect and listen without needing to "fill space" with sales fluff.

• **Loyalty and Accommodation:** Demonstrating a commitment to resident retention and service.

### Handling Common Objections

Objection

Recommended Response Strategy

**"The neighborhood feels gritty."**

Pivot to the "Growth Narrative." Acknowledge the transitional state but point to the Morrison Road redevelopment and the Light Rail investment. Frame it as being a "pioneer."

**"Why is it so cheap/10 weeks free?"**

Frame it as a strategic lease-up tool to build community momentum quickly. It is not a sign of weakness, but a reward for early adopters.

**"It's not walkable enough."**

Emphasize the "Transit Advantage." Mention the 5-minute walk to the Light Rail and the high Bike Score (70-89), offering a car-light lifestyle.

## IV. Corporate Context: Greystar & Wood Partners

Understanding the organizations behind the property is vital for the interview:

• **Greystar Real Estate Partners (Management):** The largest apartment operator in the U.S. They are performance-driven, metrics-focused, and emphasize operational excellence and resident services.

• **Wood Partners (Developer):** A national developer focused on high-quality design and identifying emerging markets.

• **The Partnership:** Wood Partners builds the "product," and Greystar provides the "execution" and management. AMH is a flagship example of this developer-operator model in Denver.

## V. Interview Preparation: Key Questions and Strategy

### Likely Interview Questions & Frameworks

1. **"Why Alta Mile High?"**

    ◦ *Focus:* Mention the unique music/arts brand and the excitement of being part of the Sun Valley growth story.

2. **"How do you describe AMH to a prospect?"**

    ◦ *Focus:* Use the phrase: "Where accessible luxury meets authentic culture." Mention the transit connectivity and the price advantage.

3. **"What is your 30/60/90 day plan?"**

    ◦ *30 Days:* Learn the Greystar systems, shadow the team, and tour all competitors.

    ◦ *60 Days:* Build resident relationships and propose a music/arts event idea.

    ◦ *90 Days:* Take full ownership of leasing targets and execution of cultural initiatives.

### Pre-Interview Research Checklist

• **Property Visit:** Walk the 5-minute route to the Decatur-Federal station to speak from experience.

• **Competitor Reconnaissance:** Drive by AMLI Broadway Park and The Dorsey to understand their "vibe" and shortcomings.

• **System Knowledge:** Familiarize yourself with the "STAR" method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for answering behavioral questions.

• **Cultural Ideas:** Have 2–3 ideas for music-themed resident events (e.g., vinyl listening nights, local artist showcases).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------


## VI. Conclusion: The "Winning Mindset"

To secure the role, the candidate must move beyond the idea of "leasing apartments" and embrace the role of a **Community Builder**. The successful leasing agent at Alta Mile High is an advocate for the neighborhood's future and a curator of its unique cultural identity. By combining Greystar’s high operational standards with a personal, "present" approach to sales, the candidate can position themselves as the ideal fit for this specific, identity-driven property.
