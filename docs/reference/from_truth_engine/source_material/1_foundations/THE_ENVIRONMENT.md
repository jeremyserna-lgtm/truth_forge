## The Entry

The spatial rules at the edge.

```
THE THRESHOLD (where Me meets Not-Me in space)

Entry Types:
├── Access Key    → moves freely through space
├── Relational    → trust grants spatial privilege
├── Others        → you go out, bring in (mediated crossing)
```

The Threshold isn't a concept—it's a **location**. The front door. The membrane at the edge of the space.

| What Enters | Spatial Behavior |
|-------------|------------------|
| Access key holder | Comes and goes. No friction at boundary. |
| Relational trust | Enters through relationship. Space opens. |
| Others | You exit, encounter, carry across. Batched entry. |

```
THE SPACE (bright, open, welcoming)

The Guardian isn't separate from the space—it's **under** it. The foundation that makes the openness structurally sound.

| Space Property | Guardian Makes Possible |
|----------------|------------------------|

| Welcoming | Because danger can be killed |

```

**Trade-off:**
- Centralized: One place to see all lenses, but adds a document
- Distributed: Each document self-declares, no extra document, but lenses are scattered

**Recommendation**: Distributed with standard header. Each collapsed document declares its lens in the same format. You see the bank by seeing any document's header.
