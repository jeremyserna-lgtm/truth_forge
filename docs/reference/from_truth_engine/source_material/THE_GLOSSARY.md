# PART I: THE PRIMITIVES

## EXIST / NOT-EXIST
**The only real question.** The binary that precedes all others. Everything else derives from this divide.

## EXIST
**To be.** The state of being rather than not-being. Not a description of what something is, but that it is.

## NOT-EXIST
**To not be.** The alternative to existence. What happens when existence stops.

## EXIST-NOW
**Existence placed in time.** The atomic unit of being. When you exist (verb) as an existence (noun), you are existing (participle). When placed in the present moment, you exist-now.

## DO-NOW
**Exist-now with direction.** Action. The doing. Existence as verb aimed at something.

---

# PART II: THE DIVIDE

## ME
**What I am.** My body, health, location, mind, thoughts, patterns. The inside of the boundary.

## NOT-ME
**What I am not.** The world, others' bodies, others' minds, ideas. What I come from. What I need to continue existing. The outside of the boundary.

## THE MEMBRANE
**The boundary between Me and Not-Me.** Not a wall but a permeable surface. Things cross it. That crossing IS life. We live in the boundary, not the rooms.

## THE DIVIDE
**The structure ME / NOT-ME.** The first division after EXIST / NOT-EXIST. You cannot have Me without Not-Me.

# PART III: THE STRUCTURE

## HOLD
**Data at rest.** What persists. What can be queried. The static state. Costs nothing while still.

## AGENT
**Transformation.** What takes input and produces output. The dynamic process. Costs while running.

## HOLD → AGENT → HOLD
**The universal structure.** All processing is: receive (HOLD), process (AGENT), deliver (HOLD). The atomic pattern of all work.

## THE PATTERN
**HOLD → AGENT → HOLD as the singular truth.** The one pattern that explains all patterns.

# PART IV: THE CYCLE

## WANT
**Care with direction.** Something matters and it points somewhere. The precondition for action. Not action itself.

## CHOOSE
**Picking from options.** Selection between possibilities. The chooser owns the outcome.

## DO
**Action.** See DO-NOW. Existence as verb.

## SEE
**Observation.** Receiving truth. Signals cross the Me/Not-Me boundary without distortion. Not passive—requires attention.

## MOVE
**Boundary crossing.** What happens after seeing. The crossing that becomes the next Want.

## THE LOOP
**WANT → CHOOSE → DO → SEE → MOVE → WANT...** The cycle of existence. Each phase feeds the next. The loop never ends.
