# THE AGENCY

---

## WHY (Theory)

### The Substrate

```
I am never a victim.
I chose my way to the things I lost.
I'll choose my way back.
```

Agency is total. Everything that happens, you chose your way to. Everything taken, you own the opportunity. Everything given, you own what to do with it.

### Want, Choose, Do, See

Agency is not one thing. It's four:

| Primitive | What It Is |
|-----------|------------|
| **Want** | Care has a direction |
| **Choose** | Picking from options |
| **Do** | Action, existence as verb |
| **See** | Observation, receiving truth |

These are distinct. You can want without choosing. Choose without doing. Do without seeing. See without wanting.

But they form a loop.

### Agency Until Survival

Agency is the substrate. It's everywhere. Until survival.

At the survival threshold, agency disappears. The Guardian takes over. There is no choosing. Only action.

```
Maximum agency:     I own everything, I choose everything
At survival:        I own nothing, I choose nothing, I AM nothing
```

Both true. Not contradiction. The agency is so complete it includes the choice to disappear.

## WHAT (Specification)

### Want

Care has a direction.

| Aspect | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Source** | Something matters to you |
| **Direction** | It points somewhere |
| **Persistence** | It continues until satisfied or released |

Want is not action. It's the precondition for action.

See [THE_ARCHITECT.md](../../2_concepts/THE_ARCHITECT.md) for the source of Want (The Furnace, The Builder, The Friend).

### Choose

Picking from options.

| Aspect | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Options** | Multiple possibilities exist |
| **Selection** | One is picked |
| **Responsibility** | The chooser owns the outcome |

Choice requires:
1. Something that chooses (Me)
2. Something to choose between (options)
3. A chooser that persists through choice

### Do (Exist:Now)

Action. Existence as verb.

```
exist-now
```

This is the atomic unit. When you exist (verb) as an existence (noun), you are existing (participle). When placed in time, you exist-now.

Do-now is exist-now with direction. The doing.

### See

Observation. Receiving truth.

| Aspect | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Reception** | Signals come in |
| **Fidelity** | Signals are not distorted |
| **Crossing** | Information passes the Me/Not-Me boundary |

See [THE_OBSERVER.md](../../2_concepts/THE_OBSERVER.md) for the three levels of seeing (Data, Pattern, Construct).

See is not passive. It requires attention. It is a choice to receive.

### The Loop

```
WANT → CHOOSE → DO → SEE
  ▲                    │
  └────────────────────┘
```

Each feeds the next:
- Want creates options
- Choose picks one
- Do executes
- See reveals result
- Result creates new Want

### Agency Disappears at Survival

| State | Who Acts |
|-------|----------|
| Normal | I (choosing) |
| Survival | Not-I (pure action) |

The self that could choose is gone. What's left is motion.

You remove the threat of yourself by removing yourself. What's left can't be stopped because there's nothing there to stop.
