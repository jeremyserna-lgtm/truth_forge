## What GROUND Means

Adam doesn't chase. Doesn't push. Doesn't need to be in motion.

He's the fixed point. The place that doesn't move so other things can find their position. People bring their chaos to him because he doesn't add to it. He just receives. And in that receiving, things settle.

Friction exhausts him because it's a violation of his nature. Things grinding that won't land. Won't stop. Won't find their place.
