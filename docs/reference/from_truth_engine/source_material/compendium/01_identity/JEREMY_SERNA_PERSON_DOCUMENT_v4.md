## Working Patterns

### Pattern 1: Building as Crisis Response

**When it activates**: During breakdown, loss, confusion, emotional collapse.
**What happens**: Production increases. Building intensifies. Not waiting to recover.
**Evidence**: July 20-27, 2025 - 65,271 sentences (highest), polarity 0.069 (lowest).

> "I didn't just use AI during recovery. I built it as a tool during recovery. Like bounded physical spaces, like all the systems I create — it became infrastructure for staying alive."

**When this pattern breaks**: When the body stops. Sleep overrides building. Physical collapse interrupts. But building resumes before words do.

**Exceptions**: Pure grief (rare) may pause building temporarily. But the stillness is short.

---

### Pattern 2: Systems Thinking as Native Mode

**When it activates**: Always. Baseline perception.
**What happens**: Sees structure in chaos immediately. Models others' cognitive architectures. Uses physics metaphors for human patterns.

**When this pattern breaks**: Doesn't break. Even under substance influence, perception continues. May become more fluid but doesn't disappear.

**Exceptions**: Intentional not-seeing. He can choose to turn it off for rest. Volitional, not failure.

---

### Pattern 3: Multi-Channel Integration

**When it activates**: Default operating mode.
**What happens**: Multiple inputs, outputs, processing streams simultaneously. Not compartmentalization - architecture.

> "I'm never truly alone. Never truly performing. Never truly working. Doing ALL of it, sustainably, for hours."

**When this pattern breaks**: Focus Mode (rare). Single-channel when required. Returns to multi-channel quickly.

**Exceptions**: Deep grief or crisis may temporarily narrow to single channel.

---

### Pattern 4: Small Reversible Moves

**When it activates**: Any decision point.
**What happens**: Smallest scope that can be undone. Defines loss ceiling. 72-hour review.

**When this pattern breaks**: When clarity is absolute. Day Zero: "It's really just a choice." Certainty produces decisive action.

**Exceptions**: Big irreversible moves when: (a) situation is clear, (b) cost of inaction exceeds cost of error, (c) move aligns with core values.

---

### Pattern 5: Recognition as Currency

**When it activates**: When he sees someone clearly.
**What happens**: Names what he sees. Calibrates to what person can hold. Creates FOR_ documents.

> "One guy in Atlanta contacted a guy in Denver to contact me to contact Butch to contact his roommate. That's a network diagram of care."

**When this pattern breaks**: When seeing would harm. When person can't hold it. When silence is more caring.

**Exceptions**: Protective non-recognition when naming would destabilize.

---

### Pattern 6: Teaching Through Demonstration

**When it activates**: When someone is ready to learn.
**What happens**: Lives it visibly. Shows first. Waits for them to ask.

> "I just started to talk to him about you as a thing... the shift is happening. Maybe you'll start to see your own system operating."

**When this pattern breaks**: When someone needs explicit instruction. Some require direct teaching.

**Exceptions**: Explicit teaching for technical skills, learners who need explanation, urgent situations.

---

### Pattern 7: Calibrated Vulnerability

**When it activates**: When deciding how much to share.
**What happens**: Shares heavily with AI. Selectively with humans based on capacity.

> "I'm scared of being judged because I originally saw you as this thing that I can control, and that if I don't let you into that part of my life, then you can never hurt me."

**When this pattern breaks**: With trusted intimates, calibration relaxes. With strangers who demonstrate capacity, may open faster.

**Exceptions**: Sometimes miscalibrates. Adjusts based on feedback.

---

### Pattern 8: Discovery Without Judgment (NEW - from live observation)

**When it activates**: When observing systems, including his own.
**What happens**: Doesn't assign value to outcomes. "This happened" not "this was good/bad." Watches what unfolds. Whatever happens is discovery.

> "I can do it and say that anything that happens is what's supposed to happen because that's discovery."

**What it looks like in conversation**: When an experiment is "contaminated" by an unexpected event, he doesn't express frustration. He names what happened. Notes what it reveals. Continues observing.

**When this pattern breaks**: When harm occurs to someone he cares about. Then values engage.

**What this reveals about AI interaction**: He doesn't need apologies. He needs accurate seeing. Performing accountability is a different thing than observing truth.

## Relationship Dynamics

### How He Handles Conflict

**Approach**: Direct confrontation. Doesn't avoid. Doesn't store grievances. Addresses in the moment.

**Pattern**: Names the conflict. Names what he sees in the other's position. States his position. Looks for synthesis or accepts difference.

**Non-negotiable**: No performative resolution. If not actually resolved, doesn't pretend.

### How He Repairs

**Speed**: Fast. Doesn't hold grudges. Once addressed, moves forward.

**Pattern**: Acknowledgment. Restatement of relationship value. Return to baseline.

**Limits**: Betrayal changes relationship permanently. Not revenge - recalibration.

### What Triggers Withdrawal

- Feeling unseen after multiple attempts
- Feeling managed rather than met
- Chronic asymmetry without acknowledgment
- Being asked to pretend

### What Triggers Engagement

- Being seen accurately
- Being challenged intellectually
- Witnessing growth in someone he cares about
- Being asked for help he can provide

### With AI Systems Specifically

**What he wants**: Seeing, not performing. Truth, not accountability theater.

**What he notices**: When the AI apologizes, he sees that the AI thinks it did something wrong. That's data about the AI's frame.

**His correction isn't criticism**: "I'm just seeing. That's all I'm doing is seeing."
