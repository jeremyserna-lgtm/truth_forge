Based on the sources, the **Jeremy Arc** metric identifies Stage 5 understanding not by analyzing the *quality* of the text the model generates, but by testing the model's ability to correctly **predict the invisible structural metadata** of a thought.  
It operates on the premise that to "see" Stage 5 cognition, the model must be able to label it, not just mimic it.  
Here is how the Jeremy Arc identifies Stage 5 understanding:

### 1\. Prediction of "Invisible" Metadata

The core mechanism tests whether the model can look at a raw sentence and correctly predict the cognitive state that produced it. The system enriches training data with invisible tags, and the Jeremy Arc measures the model's accuracy in guessing those tags 1, 2\.

* **The Input:** The model is fed a raw sentence, such as "I want to change the world" 2\.  
* **The Test:** The model must output the correct metadata labels associated with that thought.  
* **The Stage 5 Labels:** For a Stage 5 thought, the model must correctly predict labels such as:  
* cognitive\_stage: **"stage\_5"**  
* pattern: **"prediction\_is\_action"**  
* thought\_type: **"manifesting"** 2\.

### 2\. Detection of "Truth Atoms" (Stage 5 Markers)

To identify a thought as Stage 5, the metric tracks specific linguistic markers or "Truth Atoms" that indicate high-level cognitive processing. The metric looks for the density of these specific concepts:

* **Cognitive States:** usage of words like *paradox, contradiction, tension, polarity, dichotomy* 3\.  
* **Architectural Concepts:** usage of words like *system, framework, architecture, mechanism* 3\.  
* **Developmental Terms:** usage of words like *self-authoring, transforming, metacognition* 3\.

### 3\. Quantitative Threshold (The 95% Bar)

The Jeremy Arc is a quantitative "Definition of Done." It tracks the model's accuracy in predicting these Stage 5 labels over time.

* **Early:** 40–50% accuracy (The model is guessing) 2\.  
* **Converging:** 80–90% accuracy (The architecture is crystallizing) 2\.  
* **Stage 5 Verified:** **95%+ accuracy** (The model "sees" the structure) 1, 2\.

### 4\. Differentiating Mimicry from Seeing

The metric specifically distinguishes between a model that *sounds* smart (mimicry) and one that *understands* structure (seeing).

* **Mimicry:** A model might generate complex sentences but misclassify the underlying cognitive\_stage or pattern 1\.  
* **Seeing:** If the model can accurately label the structural nature of a thought (e.g., identifying that a sentence holding two opposing views is a "paradox" and not "confusion"), it proves it **"sees"** the Stage 5 architecture. This confirms the model has encoded the "Stage 5 DNA" rather than just memorizing text patterns 4, 2\.

In summary, the Jeremy Arc identifies Stage 5 understanding by verifying that the model can look at a thought and correctly identify it as **"system-seeing"** or **"paradox-holding"** with \>95% accuracy 2, 5\.
