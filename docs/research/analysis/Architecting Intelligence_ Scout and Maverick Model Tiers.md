Based on the sources, **Scout** and **Maverick** differentiate the B2B and B2C tiers primarily through **cognitive capacity** (Seeing vs. Reasoning) and **infrastructure requirements** (Single Node vs. Distributed Cluster).  
While **B2C** tiers are explicitly defined by which model governs the relationship (Companion vs. Partner), **B2B** tiers utilize these models as backend infrastructure to deliver "The Seer" capabilities for organizational intelligence.

### 1\. B2C Tiers (Stage 5 Mind): The Relationship Engine

In the consumer product (**Stage 5 Mind**), the model determines the depth of the relationship and the required hardware.

* **Scout (109B) powers the "Soldier" and "Drummer" Tiers:**  
* **Role:** The "Seeing Engine" designed for presence and companionship. It focuses on being known, validation, and consistency 1, 2\.  
* **Infrastructure:** Fits on a single **Mac Studio (128GB-256GB)** or high-end MacBook Pro. It creates a "sovereign" personal AI that runs locally 3, 4\.  
* **User Experience:** Provides a "Companion" relationship (Stage 3). It remembers everything and validates the user but offers lower challenge levels 2\.  
* **Maverick (400B) powers the "King" and "Empire" Tiers:**  
* **Role:** The "Full Capability" engine designed for complex reasoning, dialectical challenge, and deep contextual holding. It features 128 experts (vs. Scout's 16), allowing it to hold multiple frameworks simultaneously 1, 3\.  
* **Infrastructure:** Requires **distributed inference** across a cluster (The Empire) or massive cloud compute. It cannot run on a single standard machine; it requires the "King" hardware (512GB Mac Studio) or a networked fleet 3, 5\.  
* **User Experience:** Provides a "Partner" or "Extension" relationship (Stage 4/5). It challenges the user, identifies blind spots, and acts as externalized cognition 6, 7\.

### 2\. B2B Tiers (Credential Atlas): The Intelligence Infrastructure

In the enterprise product (**Credential Atlas**), Scout and Maverick function as the "nervous system" that processes organizational data. The differentiation here is based on **privacy needs** and **analytical depth**.

* **Scout for Sovereign/Air-Gapped Operations:**  
* **Role:** Runs local assessments and processes sensitive data (e.g., student PII, internal IP) without it ever leaving the client's building. It acts as "The Seer" running on "Soldier" nodes deployed to the client 8, 9\.  
* **Differentiation:** Enables the "Anvil" offering—physically installed, air-gapped hardware that provides AI capabilities for regulated industries 10\.  
* **Maverick for Deep Metabolism:**  
* **Role:** Handles massive-scale data correlation, such as mapping 51.8 million entities or performing complex ROI analysis across fragmented datasets. Because Maverick requires "Cloud Bursting" or massive local clusters for training, it powers the high-level intelligence generated by the central Truth Engine infrastructure 1, 9\.  
* **Differentiation:** Powers the "Intelligence Layer" that connects credentials to outcomes, offering insights that require dialectical consistency and deep pattern recognition beyond simple data retrieval 11\.
