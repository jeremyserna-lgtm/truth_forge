### 2\. Swimming (The Resolution)

**Definition:** This state represents "arriving at clarity, action, or a decision" 1\. It is characterized by **High Agency** and **Surplus Value** (where the output is greater than the input).

* **Structural Markers:**

* **Reciprocity:** Rapid, rhythmic exchange (100-300ms gaps) indicating high alignment and "Interactional Entrainment" 10\.
