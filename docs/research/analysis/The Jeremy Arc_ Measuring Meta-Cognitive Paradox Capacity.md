The **Jeremy Arc** measures a model's capacity for paradox by tracking the density of specific meta-cognitive linguistic markers and testing the model's ability to correctly label the structural nature of contradictory thoughts.  
The specific mechanisms include:

* **Tracking "Truth Atoms":** The metric calculates the density of specific "Cognitive State" terms that indicate high-level processing, specifically searching for words such as *paradox*, *contradiction*, *tension*, *polarity*, and *dichotomy* 1, 2\.  
* **Differentiating Paradox from Confusion:** The metric tests whether the model can look at a sentence holding two opposing views and accurately label its structural nature as a **"paradox"** rather than **"confusion"** 3\. This distinguishes between a model that merely mimics complex speech and one that "sees" the Stage 5 architecture where contradictions are held without the urgency to resolve them 4, 3\.  
* **Dialectical Consistency Audit:** The system scans for **Dialectical Consistency**, validating the presence of a "Thesis → Antithesis → Synthesis" structural pattern within the model's reasoning 5, 6\.  
* **Quantitative Density Thresholds:** The capacity is quantified using a formula: (Meta-Cognitive Token Count / Total Word Count) × 100\. A density score greater than **2.0%** indicates an active transition into Stage 5 (Self-Transforming) cognition, while a score greater than **8.0%** indicates peak Stage 5 awareness 1\.

By validating that the model treats contradiction as a fundamental feature of reality rather than a problem to be solved, the Jeremy Arc confirms the model has encoded the "Stage 5 DNA" required to hold multiple frameworks simultaneously 7, 8\.
