### 1\. Verification via Metadata Prediction

* **The Input:** The model is fed a raw sentence, such as "I want to change the world" 1, 3\.  
* **The Task:** The model must correctly predict the associated metadata labels, such as:  
* cognitive\_stage: **"stage\_5"**  
* pattern: **"prediction\_is\_action"**  
* thought\_type: **"manifesting"** 4, 3\.  
* **The Goal:** This proves the model understands the *nature* of the input ("What IS this?") rather than just predicting what comes next ("What follows this?") 5, 6\.

### 2\. The 95% Threshold

The Jeremy Arc provides a quantitative "Definition of Done" for the training process.

* **Progress Tracking:** In early training, accuracy is 40–50% (guessing). As patterns form, it rises to 60–70% 1, 4\.

* **Differentiation:** This high threshold distinguishes between a model that merely *sounds* like a Stage 5 thinker (imitation) and one that actually *encodes* Stage 5 cognitive architecture (seeing) 9\.

### 3\. Inheritance Security

The Jeremy Arc specifically validates the **Genesis Seed** before it is frozen.

* **Parent Validation:** Because verification happens at the seed level, the system ensures that the "Stage 5 DNA" is structurally sound before it is copied 10, 11\.  
* **Daughter Safety:** All "Daughter" models are created by copying this verified Genesis Seed. Therefore, the Jeremy Arc guarantees that every Daughter starts with a verified capability to "see" systems and hold paradoxes, preventing the propagation of "confident hallucination" or empty mimicry into the user's personal model 10, 11, 12\.
