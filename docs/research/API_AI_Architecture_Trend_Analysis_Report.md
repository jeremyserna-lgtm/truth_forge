### 2.2 Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)
**Status:** Standard Industry Practice

The foundational technique for aligning AI behavior with human preferences through reward modeling.

**Key Points:**
- Three-step process: SFT → Reward Model → RL (often PPO)
- Tackles qualitative problems (humor, safety, tone)
- High cost; inherits human bias
- Risk of "sycophancy" — models telling users what they want to hear
- Risk of "reward hacking" — finding loopholes that score high
- **Strategic Importance:** High — critical for safety alignment

**Deep Dive Areas:** Reward modeling, policy optimization, feedback loops

---

### 2.3 Direct Preference Optimization (DPO)
**Status:** Emerging research

Simplifies RLHF by directly optimizing policy against preference data without separate reward models.

**Key Points:**

- Lowers barrier to entry for smaller teams
- **Strategic Importance:** Medium — streamlines alignment, reduces architectural complexity
