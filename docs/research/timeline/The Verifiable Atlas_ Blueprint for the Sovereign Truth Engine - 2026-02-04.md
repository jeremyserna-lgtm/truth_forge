## 2. The Perceptual Apparatus: Building the Enrichment Pipeline

High-fidelity data ingestion is the prerequisite for a master timeline that functions as a Truth Engine. "Data Liberation" is the act of extracting information from fragmented silos—PDFs, dialogue logs, and archival fragments—to transmute them into a unified, actionable stream. The goal is to move from "Raw Data" to "Systemic Vision" by ensuring that every interaction is treated as refactorable source code.

The perceptual workflow is modeled on the **iRead+** and **MACAON** pipelines, which prioritize the preservation of original markup (the "Layout") while adding layers of "Semantic Annotation." For linguistic processing, the system employs specific, robust toolsets: **Frog** for Dutch (utilizing memory-based processing) and **TreeTagger** for French (integrated with the fine-grained **GRACE** tag set).

The system operates through two primary cognitive functions:

1. **The Eye (Observation):** Utilizes WARC-GPT and OCR to harvest data, ensuring that "what can be seen, is seen," effectively dismantling the fenced territories of information silos.

2. **The Mind (Patterning):**

    ◦ **Dialogue Act Classification:** Categorizing utterances (e.g., STATEMENT, QUESTION, AGREEMENT, APOLOGY) to recognize the "Act" within the "Word"—the illocutionary force of the Architect's speech acts.

    ◦ **Named Entity Disambiguation (NED):** Using Bayesian inference and the Knowledge Base (DBpedia, Wiktionary, Cornetto) to assign unique URI keys to every entity. This transforms nebulous mentions into permanent, verifiable anchors in the Atlas.

To bridge the gap between nebulous desire and executable need, the system utilizes LLMs constrained by recursive JSON Schemas.
