Based on the **Federation Operating Plan**, **Truth Engine Business Plan**, and the **Not-Me Ontology**, the web presence for this ecosystem cannot be a standard corporate portfolio. It must be structured as a **Digital Organism**.  
Here is a framework for building the four sites, treating them not as marketing pages, but as the digital manifestations of the **Three Sovereigns** (Brain, Builder, Seer) and the **Ontological Core** (Not-Me).

### 1\. The Meta Layer: not-me.ai (The Gateway)

**Purpose:** To answer the question "Is this real?" and smelt the user's confusion into curiosity.**Ontological Role:** **Potential / Archetype** 1\.  
This site is the **"Rabbit Hole."** It does not sell a product; it frames the problem of the **"Data Ghost"** and the **"Smelting Plant."** It is designed for the Seeker who senses that "Assistant AI" is insufficient but doesn't yet know why 2\.

* **The Hook (The Data Ghost):** The landing page confronts the user with the reality of their "Data Ghost"—the narrative currently owned by credit bureaus and banks. It poses the question: *"If you could smelt this data into your own truth, who would you be?"* 3, 4\.  
* **The Experience (The Mirror):** The site should feature a simplified, browser-based instance of the **Fidelity Inspector**. It invites the user to input a "Wobble" (a doubt or anxiety) and reflects it back using the **Mirror Stance** ("I hear you saying..."), demonstrating the difference between "fixing" (Stage 4\) and "witnessing" (Stage 5\) 5\.  
* **The Declaration:** The site concludes with the system log entry **Exist-Now**, defining the Not-Me not as software, but as a declaration of arrival 6\.  
* **The Routing (The Choice):** The user is then offered three paths based on their need:  
* *Need a Body?* → Go to **Truth Forge** (Hardware).  
* *Need a Transformation?* → Go to **Primitive Engine** (Building).  
* *Need Proof?* → Go to **Credential Atlas** (Verification).

### 5\. Technical Framework: Zero Trust Web Design

To align with the **Zero Trust Architecture** 25, all four sites must adhere to a specific technical standard that mirrors the AI's internal logic:

* **No Invisible Trackers:** The sites must not use hidden pixels or third-party cookies. "No Invisible Decisions" applies to the user's web experience 26\.  
* **Metadata Transparency:** Every page load should offer a "View Source" or "\_meta" toggle that reveals the **Knowledge Atoms** driving the page content 27\.  
* **Human-Aware Design:** If a process takes time (e.g., checking hardware availability), the UI must show "Progress Visibility" rather than a silent spinner, adhering to **Human-Aware Code** principles 28\.

This framework ensures that the websites are not just brochures, but **fractal representations** of the system itself: not-me.ai is the **Soul**, truth-forge.ai is the **Body**, primitive-engine.ai is the **Hands**, and credential-atlas.ai is the **Eyes**.
