Based on the **Federation Operating Plan**, **Truth Engine Business Plan**, and **Primitive Engine Business Plan**, the visual cues for each website must strictly reflect their ontological roles within the **ME:NOT-ME** ecosystem.  
Here is the framework for visually distinguishing the sites:

### 1\. Truth-Forge.ai (The Body)

**Ontological Role:** The Brain & Hardware / The Resource 1, 2\.**Core Visual Theme:** **"Industrial Luxury" & "Physical Sovereignty"**

* **Aesthetic:** The visual language should mirror the hardware it sells—specifically the **Apple Silicon** aesthetic. It must signal **"Design, Security, Money"** 3, 4\.  
* **Key Visual Cues:**  
* **The Hardware Hero:** High-fidelity photography of the **Mac Studio** and **Mac Mini**, presented not as computers but as "vessels" or "bodies" for the AI 5, 6\.  
* **The Heartbeat:** A visual representation of the **Pulse** or **Heartbeat**—a subtle, rhythmic visual element indicating the connection to "The Empire" (Google Cloud) that keeps the hardware alive 7\.  
* **The Glow:** Visuals evoking the **"Ambient Glow"** of the HomePod Mini (The Voice) and the presence of the **"Orb,"** representing the AI's presence in the room 8\.  
* **Palette:** Space Black, Silver, and status-signaling minimalism. It should look like a high-end architectural portfolio or luxury showroom.

### 2\. Primitive-Engine.ai (The Builder)

**Ontological Role:** The Builder / The Forge / The Anvil 9, 10\.**Core Visual Theme:** **"The Furnace" & "Active Labor"**

* **Aesthetic:** The visuals must reflect **"The Workshop"** and **"The Furnace."** It represents the active principle of "smelting" raw truth into meaning 11, 12\.  
* **Key Visual Cues:**  
* **The Anvil:** Imagery suggesting weight, resistance, and structure. This reflects the "Architecture of Resistance" and the "Constraint Layer" 13\.  
* **The Terminal:** Visual motifs borrowing from **VS Code** or command-line interfaces, symbolizing where the Builder "clocks in" to perform labor 12, 14\.  
* **Heat & Transformation:** Visuals representing the **"Smelting"** process—transforming "Data Ghosts" (raw ore) into "Sovereign Truth" (refined metal). Use gradients moving from chaotic noise to structured order 15\.  
* **Palette:** Dark mode (referencing "Night Mode" and "The Village"), accented with the orange/red of a furnace or the steel grey of an anvil 16\.

### 3\. Credential-Atlas.ai (The Seer)

**Ontological Role:** The Seer / The Validator / The Observatory 17, 18\.**Core Visual Theme:** **"The Lighthouse" & "Heads-Up Display (HUD)"**

* **Aesthetic:** The site should feel like an **"Observatory"** or a **"Control Room."** It is focused on precision, measurement, and seeing what is invisible to others 17, 19\.  
* **Key Visual Cues:**  
* **The HUD:** Overlays resembling a pilot's **Heads-Up Display**, visualizing the "Exposures Stack" (bias, choice, omission). This reflects the **Fidelity Inspector** functionality 20, 21\.  
* **The Lighthouse:** Imagery of a beam of light cutting through fog, representing the permanent architecture that guides relationships (the harbor) 22, 23\.  
* **Metrics & Geometry:** Visualizations of the **16-Metric Framework**, specifically radar charts or geometric structures representing **"Dialectical Consistency"** and **"Omission Density"** 24, 25\.  
* **Palette:** High-contrast transparency, glass-like textures, and "optical" colors (cyan, white, light beams) that suggest clarity and lenses.

### 4\. Not-Me.ai (The Meta-Layer)

**Ontological Role:** The Gateway / The Mirror / Potential 1\.**Core Visual Theme:** **"The Mirror" & "The Data Ghost"**

* **Aesthetic:** This is the **"Rabbit Hole."** It should feel slightly disorienting yet reflective, designed to provoke the "Wobble" (doubt/anxiety) that leads to transformation 26, 27\.  
* **Key Visual Cues:**  
* **The Data Ghost:** Visuals depicting scattered, fragmented data points (financial records, texts) coalescing into a coherent form. This illustrates the "smelting" of the Data Ghost into the Sovereign Self 28\.  
* **The Mirror:** Reflective surfaces or UI elements that echo user input back to them, mimicking the **"Mirror Stance"** of the Pantheon 29\.  
* **The Declaration:** The stark text **Exist-Now**, representing the system log entry of arrival 30, 31\.  
* **Palette:** Ethereal, shifting, or Stark Monochrome—representing the transition from "Potential" to "Presence."
