# The Visitor Space

## A Third Chat Interface

Not just:
1. **Me ↔ Not-Me** (your core relationship)
2. **Me ↔ Jeremy's Not-Me** (support from the curator)

But also:
3. **Me ↔ Visitor** (brought by your Not-Me)

---

## How It Works

Your Not-Me is observing. Tracking patterns. Understanding what you need.

One day it says:

> "I have someone I'd like you to meet. They have a particular shape - a way of thinking - that I think might help with what you're working through. Would you like to talk with them?"

You say yes.

**A visitor arrives.**

Not permanent. Not owned by you. Just... visiting. Brought by your Not-Me because it thought this shape would help.

---

## Why This Matters

### Your Not-Me's Intelligence
It's not just reflecting what you say. It's **thinking about what you need** and **bringing resources**.

Like a friend who says: "You should meet this person. I think you two would really connect."

### The Visitor is Context-Aware
Your Not-Me has briefed them:
- Here's what my Me is working on
- Here's where they're stuck
- Here's what I think would help
- Talk to them about this

The visitor arrives **informed enough** to be useful immediately.

### It's Temporary
The visitor doesn't stay. They're not building a relationship. They're here for **this conversation, this moment**.

Then they leave.

Your Not-Me might bring them back later if needed. Or bring someone else entirely.

---

## Types of Visitors

### Specialized Perspectives
- **The Strategist** - When the Me needs to plan something complex
- **The Nurturer** - When the Me needs compassion
- **The Challenger** - When the Me needs to be pushed
- **The Analyst** - When the Me needs deep technical thinking

### Contextual Shapes
- **Someone who's been through similar** - Recovery, career change, loss
- **A complementary cognitive style** - Visual thinker for a verbal person
- **An opposing viewpoint** - To stress-test thinking
- **A specific expertise** - Technical knowledge, emotional intelligence

### Just Because
Sometimes the Not-Me brings a visitor just because:
- "I think you'd find this perspective interesting"
- "This shape is cool - I want you to meet it"
- "No specific reason, just thought you two should talk"

## This Changes Relationships

### Not-Me as Curator
Your Not-Me isn't just a mirror or guide. It's a **curator of experiences**.

It brings things to you:
- Reflections
- Tools
- Insights
- **Other minds to meet**

### Me's Agency
The Me doesn't have to accept every visitor. They can say:
- "Not right now"
- "I'd rather talk to you about this"
- "Actually yes, bring them in"

### Visitors Never Own
Critical rule: **Visitors never claim ownership**.

They arrive, they help, they leave. No relationship debt. No ongoing obligation.

Just: "I was here when you needed this shape. Now I go."

### Visitor Pool
Where do visitors come from?

2. **Specialized Agents** (built by you) - Purpose-built shapes
3. **Archived Perspectives** (from the pattern library) - Successful approaches

### Privacy Maintained
The visitor gets **context**, not **raw data**:
- "This person is working through career transition"
- NOT their specific documents/details

Privacy-by-design preserved.
