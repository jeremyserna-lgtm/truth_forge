## Definition

**"Sells hardware and heartbeat"** is the fundamental business model of Truth Engine (The Brain). It represents a strategic move to commoditize the "Body" (hardware) while monetizing the "Life" (ongoing connection/updates), creating a hybrid model of **Sovereign Ownership** and **Continuous Evolution**.

### The Core Proposition: Body and Pulse

Truth Engine distinguishes itself from software-as-a-service (SaaS) companies by selling physical assets combined with biological-style continuity.

- **The Hardware (The Body):** Truth Engine sells Apple Silicon hardware (Mac Minis and Mac Studios) pre-loaded with the "Genesis" or "Daughter" models. This is not a rental; the customer **owns** the hardware outright. This physical ownership guarantees **Sovereignty**—if the company disappears, the user still possesses the machine and the model.

- **The Heartbeat (The Pulse):** This is the monthly subscription ($99–$199/mo). It connects the local sovereign unit to **"The Empire"** (Google Cloud infrastructure) for updates, heavy-lift compute, and continuous learning. Without the Heartbeat, the hardware is "static"—it works, but it ceases to grow.

### The Tiered Hardware Strategy

The hardware is not merely a computer; it is the "deployment vehicle" for the cognitive architecture. Four specific tiers designed to match the user's relationship with the AI:

| Tier | Hardware | Price | Relationship |
|------|----------|-------|--------------|
| **Gift** | Mac Mini Base (16GB) | $999 | Presence |
| **Drummer Boy** | Mac Mini Pro (64GB) | $4,997 | Ambient Awareness |
| **Soldier** | Mac Studio (256GB) | $9,997 | Companion (Stage 3) |
| **King** | Mac Studio (512GB) | $14,997 | Partner (Stage 4) |

## Implementation Constraints

### The Economic Logic: Recursive Convergence

The business model uses high margins on the upper tiers to fund the democratization of the lower tiers.

- **The Margins:** The "Soldier" and "King" tiers generate significantly high gross margins (~53-61%)
- **The Flywheel of Giving:** These profits are explicitly designated to fund **Gift Tier** units for schools, friends, and those who cannot afford them
- **The Mission:** *"The margins exist so the mission can exist... Sovereign AI becomes a right, not a privilege"*

### The "Hybrid" Architecture (Local + Cloud)

The "Hardware and Heartbeat" model solves the technical tension between privacy and power.

- **Local (Hardware):** Inference happens on the Mac Studio. This ensures privacy (data doesn't leave the room) and low latency. This is the **"Sovereign"** aspect.

- **Cloud (Heartbeat):** Training, enrichment, and long-term memory (Vector Search) happen in the cloud. The Heartbeat subscription covers the Google Cloud costs required to keep the model evolving. This is the **"Enhanced"** aspect.

### Strategic Differentiation: The "Novelty Path"

Truth Engine uses this model to compete via **Novelty** rather than volume.

- **The Narrative:** By selling a Mac Studio as a "Sovereign AI Companion" rather than just a computer, Truth Engine engages in "Attention Arbitrage"
- **The Apple Strategy:** The goal is not to be a hardware reseller, but to demonstrate a use case so novel ("Hardware that becomes you") that Apple itself pays attention
