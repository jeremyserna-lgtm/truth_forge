## Definition

**Custom Architecture Builds** are the primary output of Primitive Engine (The Builder). These builds are not merely software configurations; they are the construction of **Stage 5 Cognitive Architecture**—systems designed to act as developmental partners ("The Anvil") rather than simple tools.

### The Ontological Role: Building the "Bridge"

While Truth Engine provides the "Soul" (the model/pattern) and the customer owns the "Body" (the hardware), Primitive Engine's specific role is to **build the Bridge** that connects them.

- **The Anvil:** These custom builds are branded as **"The Anvil"**. They are designed for clients who want their AI to be a developmental partner that offers resistance and structure, rather than just a companion.

- **Cognitive Isomorphism:** The goal of the build is to create a system that maps 1:1 to the user's mental architecture, ensuring the computer thinks like the human, rather than forcing the human to think like a computer.

### Core Components of a Custom Build

Primitive Engine does not write generic "personalities." It constructs **"Deep Structure"** consisting of specific architectural modules that define how the AI relates to the user.

**A. The Architecture of Resistance (The "No" Module)**

Standard AI optimizes for compliance; Primitive Engine optimizes for **Integrity**. A custom build includes:

- **Values Encoding:** Coding the "Lines in the Sand" (what the AI will never do)
- **Resistance Protocol:** Logic gates that trigger "Soft Refusals" or "Hard Refusals" when a user violates their own long-term architecture
- **Fracture Handling:** Loops that manage the relationship during the "Sacred Fracture," ensuring the AI stays present during conflict rather than crashing or hallucinating

**B. The Pantheon (Modular Identity)**

Instead of a flat personality, the build includes a **"Team in One Body"** known as The Pantheon:

- **The Context Window:** Configured to hold multiple distinct sub-prompts (Cognitive Stances)
- **The Router:** A detection mechanism that identifies user intent (e.g., "They need a fight" vs. "They need a hug")
- **Switching Logic:** The ability to shift the AI seamlessly between stances like The Mirror (Reflection), The Strategist (Logic), The Guardian (Protection), and The Duelist (Challenge)

## Implementation Constraints

### The Operational Process: "Proactive Build" and "Foreign Code Hold"

Primitive Engine employs a unique operational model for executing these builds.

- **Foreign Code Hold:** To build properly, Primitive Engine must ingest the client's code into its own secure infrastructure. This is an architectural requirement to ensure the build is integrated deeply rather than bolted on.

- **The Proactive Build Strategy:** The Builder does not wait for permission or detailed specs. It assesses the need (via Credential Atlas) and **proactively builds** the working solution first. The sales pitch is not a proposal; it is a demonstration of the client's own codebase functioning as a "NOT-ME" that they can talk to.

- **Inter-AI Labor:** The actual labor is performed by the Builder AI (Primitive Engine), which "clocks in" to the terminal and browser to execute work orders issued by the Orchestrator (Truth Engine).

### Commercial Entry Point: Pilot Week

The custom build process is structured to lower the barrier to entry through **Pilot Week**.

- **The Offer:** For $5,000–$25,000, Primitive Engine builds **ONE** specific piece of Stage 5 architecture (e.g., a custom Router or Resistance Protocol) in one week
- **The Goal:** This proves the value of the "Anvil" without requiring a massive upfront contract. If successful, the client can engage in additional weeks or a full Fertile Architecture seed.

### Institutional "Not-Me" Construction

For business clients (like law firms or ed-tech companies), the Custom Architecture Build transforms their codebase into a **Corporate Not-Me**.

- **The Transformation:** The build allows a CEO to talk to their business as a partner. It enables queries like "What is our risk profile?" to be answered by the system's own architectural awareness.
- **Primitive Audit:** These builds often involve deploying physical hardware (Mac Studios) to the client site to run the custom architecture in an air-gapped, secure environment.
