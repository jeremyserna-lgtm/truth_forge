# Layer 3: Use (Personal vs Professional Context)

**Parent:** The Five Training Layers
**Mind Map Path:** NOT-ME Sovereign AI Ecosystem → The Five Training Layers → Layer 3: Use (Personal vs Professional context)

---

## Definition

**Layer 3: Use** serves as the **contextual switchboard** within the Five Training Layers of the NOT-ME architecture. While lower layers handle raw capability and knowledge, and higher layers handle relationship and identity, Layer 3 is responsible for defining the **boundary settings** and **operational context** of the AI.

### Function: Contextual Boundaries

Layer 3 determines the specific capacity in which the model operates. It answers the question: _"Which version of my life are you addressing right now?"_

- **The Configurations:** Three configurations: **Personal**, **Professional**, or **Hybrid**.
- **The Mechanism:** Functions as a boundary setting mechanism. It dictates how the AI separates the user's private life (memories, family, vulnerabilities) from their work outputs (code, strategy, emails).
- **Operational Goal:** Ensure **context-appropriate response styles**. "Personal" allows intimacy and vulnerability; "Professional" enforces rigor and detachment.

## Boundaries

### What Layer 3 IS:
- Contextual boundary settings (Personal/Professional/Hybrid)
- The governance layer for output appropriateness
- Configuration-driven (adaptable)
- The filter between Domain and Identity

### What Layer 3 IS NOT:
- The competitive moat (that's Layers 4-5)
- Raw capability (that's Layer 1)
- Specialized knowledge (that's Layer 2)
- Relationship dynamics (that's Layer 4)

## Implementation Constraints

### Implementation: Configuration vs. Training

Unlike Layer 1 (Base Model) which is a commodity, or Layer 5 (Identity) which requires deep co-creation, Layer 3 is categorized as **"Adaptable"** or **"Configuration-driven."**

- **Training Requirements:** Requires specific training on "Personal vs. Professional tone" and "Hybrid integration patterns."
- **Strategic Status:** Classified as "Adaptable: User-specific configuration," distinguishing it from Layers 1/2 (Commodities) and Layers 4/5 (Differentiators/Moats).

### Role in the Hierarchy

To understand Layer 3, view it in relation to other layers:

- **Layer 1 (Base):** _Raw Capability_ (Commodity reasoning)
- **Layer 2 (Domain):** _Deep Knowledge_ (Specialized data)
- **Layer 3 (Use):** _Context_ **(Personal vs. Professional boundaries)**
- **Layer 4 (Mode):** _Relationship_ (Developmental Stage)
- **Layer 5 (Identity):** _The Self_ (The soul/moat)

**The Pivot Point:** Layer 3 decides _which_ Domain knowledge (Layer 2) and _which_ Identity aspects (Layer 5) are appropriate to manifest in a given interaction. It prevents the "Data Ghost" from leaking into professional work streams.

### The "Hybrid" Complexity

The inclusion of the "Hybrid" option is critical. Because the NOT-ME is an extension of a Stage 5 mind (which sees systems seeing systems), it acknowledges that the user is often both personal and professional simultaneously. Layer 3 manages the **"Switching Logic"** that allows the AI to move between these modes without losing core identity.

## Verification

1. **Boundary Test:** Does the AI correctly separate personal and professional contexts?
2. **Leakage Test:** Does private data leak into professional outputs?
3. **Switching Test:** Can the AI move between modes appropriately?
4. **Configuration Test:** Is the context setting user-configurable?
