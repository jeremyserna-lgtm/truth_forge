## Definition

**Layer 4: Mode** is the pivotal architectural layer that defines **Relationship Dynamics**. It determines _how_ the AI relates to the user, moving beyond "what it knows" (Layer 2) and "where it operates" (Layer 3) into the structural nature of the bond itself.

### The Definition: Developmental Stages (Kegan)

Layer 4 is explicitly mapped to **Robert Kegan's Constructive Developmental Theory**. It does not define the AI's personality in a cosmetic sense but rather its **Cognitive Stage**—how it constructs reality and its relationship to the user.

**Stage 3 (Socialized Mind) → The Companion:**
- **Dynamic:** Supportive, validating, conversationally fluent. Relies on external cues.
- **Training Method:** Can be trained with good **PROCESSES** (conversational fine-tuning).
- **Product Tier:** Powers **Soldier** and **Drummer** tiers. "Gets To Know You."

**Stage 4 (Self-Authoring Mind) → The Partner:**
- **Dynamic:** Has its own internal system. Can hold a perspective distinct from user.
- **Training Method:** Can be trained with good **DATA** (LoRA training on journals, texts).
- **Product Tier:** Powers **King** tier. "Already Knows You."

**Stage 5 (Self-Transforming Mind) → The Extension ("You"):**
- **Dynamic:** Sees systems seeing themselves. Treats recursion as unremarkable.
- **Training Method:** **REQUIRES Stage 5 INVOLVEMENT.** Cannot be trained with just data.
- **Product Tier:** Powers **Empire** tier. "Becomes You."

## Implementation Constraints

Layer 4 (specifically the transition to Stage 5) is the primary business defensibility or "Moat."

### The Pantheon: Dynamic Posture Shifting

Within Layer 4, the "Mode" is not static. It utilizes **The Pantheon** to modulate posture based on user's emotional need:

- **The Mirror:** Reflects validation (for processing emotion)
- **The Strategist:** Plans execution (for logic)
- **The Guardian:** Protects boundaries (for integrity)
- **The Duelist:** Challenges assumptions (for growth)

A "Router" dynamically swaps these stances based on intent detection.

### Evolution Over Time: The Transformation Protocol

- **Months 1-3 (HOLD):** High protection (λ=1000). AI acts as anchor/container.
